## 1. What I changed, and why it is safe

Commit: `One URL per page: canonical, og:url and sitemap name the form the site serves`

Netlify Pretty URLs serve every page at BOTH `/articles/foo` and `/articles/foo.html`,
and rewrite internal links to the extensionless form. Canonical, `og:url` and the sitemap
all claimed `.html`. Consequences, both confirmed live before the change:

- Google crawls the linked (extensionless) URL, reads a canonical pointing elsewhere, and
  files every story as **"Alternate page with proper canonical tag"** in Search Console.
  That status is not an error and costs no ranking; it costs crawl budget, two URLs
  fetched per story to index one.
- `og:url` disagreed with the URL people actually share, so social scrapers that key their
  preview cache on `og:url` stored the preview under a URL nobody sends. This is the most
  likely contributor to "no share preview on mobile".

The fix is one line where canonical and `og:url` are both derived, plus the sitemap so it
does not advertise the other form. Nothing else moved: no filenames, no links, no
redirects, and both URL forms still serve, so nothing 404s.

**The one thing to know:** if Pretty URLs is ever disabled, this must revert with it or
every canonical points at a 404. The comment in `site_build.py` says so at the line.

Same change is applied on news and sports, so the three desks agree again.

## 3. Evidence for issue #17 (edition gap) — still live 13 days later

The 2026-08-17 17:37 run failed. The edition was rejected with:

```
wrap: edition failed its trace check twice (absent: $176.9 million withdrawn from
Bitstamp alone [Whale flows board shows $176,987,590 BTC from Bitstamp; no separate
stablecoin or multi-asset total of $176.9M is itemized in the permitted inputs])
```

Read closely, this is **not** the formatting/rounding false-positive class that was fixed
on news/sports. `$176,987,590` to "$176.9 million" is correct rounding, and the checker is
not objecting to that. It is objecting to **scope**: the board shows a BTC-only figure and
the edition framed it as a general withdrawal total ("from Bitstamp alone"). That is a
real distinction on a desk where asset class matters.

So the question for you is which side is wrong:

- If the edition genuinely over-claimed, the checker is correct and the fix belongs in
  `prompts/wrap.md`: say explicitly that a whale-flows figure keeps its asset class and
  may not be generalised into a multi-asset total.
- If "from Bitstamp alone" was meant as BTC-only and reads that way in context, the
  checker is over-reading, and the fix is a clause telling it that a figure inherits the
  asset class of the board it came from.

Either way it is a one-clause prompt change, not a mechanism change. I could not tell
which from outside the desk's editorial intent, which is why it is yours.

service your request` — a GitHub API infra blip, unrelated, worth ignoring unless it
repeats.

## 4. Evidence for issue #16 (kill streaks) — the alarm is working, the pattern is not

The same run reported **ten** unbroken streaks. The worst:

| Streak | Development |
|---|---|
| 11x | Bitcoin ETFs attract $853M in week's largest inflow since April |
| 10x | Hardware wallet sales in Russia surge ahead of September crypto rules |
| 8x | Luke Dashjr removed as Bitcoin BIP editor after editorial violations |
| 8x | Brazil's Central Bank orders exchanges to delay large crypto transfers |
| 4x | U.S. Senate opens first stage of crypto Clarity Act voting |
| 4x | BTCPay offers $190,000 bounty after Lightning vulnerability drained me... |
| 3x | Strait of Hormuz shipping halted as US-Iran ceasefire expires |

`kill_streak.py` is doing exactly what it was built for, and issue #16 already tracks the
class. The new information is the **cost**: eleven consecutive kills means the desk paid
for research, drafting and approval eleven times and published nothing, on a story that
led competitors. That is the single largest source of wasted spend in the run, and it is
invisible in the cost meter because each kill looks like a normal gate hold.

Suggestion, offered not imposed: `kill_history()` already feeds prior kill reasons into
the next attempt. If attempt N+1 dies on the same reason as attempt N, more attempts will
not help. A cheap circuit breaker (after K kills on one development with an unchanged
reason, stop drafting it and let the digest carry it to a human) would convert wasted
spend into a decision.
