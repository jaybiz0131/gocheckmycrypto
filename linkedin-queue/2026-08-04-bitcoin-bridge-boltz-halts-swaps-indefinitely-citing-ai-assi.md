Boltz, the non-custodial swap bridge handling Lightning and Liquid payments across Bull Bitcoin, Aqua, and ZEUS wallets, suspended all swap services indefinitely on August 3 after months of AI-assisted attacks outpaced the team's ability to patch vulnerabilities.

The shutdown underscores a structural problem: open-source teams operating at modest scale face attackers who iterate faster with automated tools. User funds remain secure under Boltz's atomic swap design, but the incident reveals how security asymmetry can disable infrastructure even when code is sound.
