## A. Program 8: readers. GoCheckMyCrypto, October 14, 2026

### 1. Why
Programs 6 and 7 made the site right: one look, honest charts, a morning and an evening Brief, and a fact-checker that keeps true sentences and stops false pairings. Nothing yet brings a stranger to it. Program 8 gives the site doors: pages that answer what people actually search, built from numbers and reads the desk already produces, at no new model cost.

### 2. The doors
1. **The daily answer.** "Why is crypto down today" and "why is crypto up today" are among the most repeated crypto searches. They spike on the days that matter, and most answers are hype or a week old.
   - The site answers it every day, twice, from the morning read and the evening Edition.
   - It lives at one living page, /today/, and a dated archive page per day, /today/YYYY-MM-DD.
   - The title and headline take the day's real direction from Bitcoin's 24h change: "Why is crypto down today? Wednesday, October 14, 2026" or "...up today?"
   - A flat day (Bitcoin within plus or minus 0.5 percent) reads "Crypto today: flat".
2. **The coin doors.** Each coin page's title is what people type: "Bitcoin price today and why it moved".
   - Its description comes from the day's own numbers.
   - The top 20 coins get a "Why <coin> moved today" block from the wire items that name it, with sources, or nothing.
3. **The share loop.** Every evening the build renders square cards of the Brief and the Board for Jack to post. Every daily page gets its own Open Graph card.
4. **The measurement.** A search baseline now, and a read every Monday.

### 3. The promise kept
- **Fact-based, no hype.** The daily answer says what moved, by how much, in what window, and which reported events sit beside it, each with its source.
- **It never predicts.** It never says "because" unless a source says so. It never gives advice. The copy law, the twins gate and color truth hold on every new page.
- **No new model calls.** The daily answer is built from the morning read, the Edition, the snapshot and the wire.
- **Builds:** the morning and evening builds carry the updates, so the five a day do not change.

### 4. The sprints, one session each
- **8.1, the daily answer** (this session, Sonnet medium): /today/ and the dated archive, the 7.x cleanup, the cadence line.
- **8.2, the coin doors** (Sonnet medium):
  - search titles and descriptions on every coin page
  - the "why it moved" block on the top 20
  - BreadcrumbList structured data
  - sitemap lastmod from real change times
  - IndexNow (new on Crypto; the key file is served from the site, no account needed)
- **8.3, the share loop** (Sonnet medium): the evening Brief and Board cards, per-page Open Graph cards for /today/, and the queue Jack posts from (never posted for him).
- **8.4, the first read** (Sonnet low, documents only): the Search Console baseline, the queries the doors were built for, and the first Monday read.

### 5. Measurement, and the kill test
The Monday Search Console read adds three Crypto lines:
- impressions and clicks on queries containing "why is crypto", "crypto down", "crypto up", "today", and each top-20 coin name with "price" or "why"
- /today/ and coin pages indexed against crawled-not-indexed
- the share cards' clicks, where the platform reports them

The first read that can mean anything is November 9, three weeks after 8.2's pages index. If the December 7 read shows no impressions growth on those queries, the daily page is frozen (its URLs kept) and the desk's time goes elsewhere. The coin doors stay either way.
