## 1. What the read found

### The site is two sites

The home page is Program 5's design: paper, ink and rule red, Newsreader for headlines, Plex Mono for figures, Inter for labels. Every other page is the older design: a pink and peach gradient wash behind everything, a photo hero on the Board (/pulse), big serif intro paragraphs, and a persona voice ("The desk's technician reads the boards... He has one rule, carved over his door"). The old brand name survives too: /bottom-line's browser title is "The Bottom Line - Crypto Cronkite", and the share card's alt text says "Crypto Cronkite share card". A reader moving from the home page to any other page lands in a different publication. This is most of what reads as a mess.

### The home page is cluttered

- Every tile carries four or five lines of small print in three typefaces. Program 5's tile law allows the label, the figure, the window, one since-yesterday line, and the source with its stamp, and no explainer.
- The same numbers repeat. Fear & Greed appears three times on the home page (since yesterday, the mood, the Board). Funding and the stablecoin float appear twice each.
- Bitcoin's day change shows three ways at once: +0.36% on the live tape, +0.53% in the narrative band, and +0.52% in since yesterday. Each one is correct for its own moment, but to a reader the site disagrees with itself.
- The tape's seven-day line is a thin line with no scale, and its low and high sit far from it.

### The charts are weak

The coin page chart (/coins/btc) is small, with axis labels too small to read on a phone, and it has no 1D view. The Chart Master's charts are the same. Program 5 asked for the data to look like it belongs in a game, the standard GoCheckMyWeather and Vital set. Today it looks like a spreadsheet's default chart.

### The numbers are right

- Bitcoin on the coin page was $83,005.00 at 10:07 AM ET against CoinGecko's $83,007, and the 24-hour change was +0.36% against CoinGecko's +0.355%.
- Fear & Greed in the snapshot was 61, which matches alternative.me's reading for today.
- The snapshot was stamped 9:10 AM ET today.

The data layer that Program 5 built is sound. The problem is presentation, plus three stale pieces:

1. The Board's headline is the Chart Master's read of October 8. It has refused to publish on October 9 and 10, so a three-day-old read sits on top of today's Board.
2. An old curated calendar (data/week_calendar.json, newest entry 24 days old) still runs beside the new calendar.json. Its staleness check fails the daily brief's run and sends Jack error emails.
3. Spot ETF net shows October 9's figure on Sunday. The desk should confirm whether Farside has published October 10's yet, or whether the read is behind.

### Navigation and names are tangled

- On a phone the menu scrolls sideways and News is cut off at the edge.
- One thing has three names: the nav says "The Edition", the URL is /bottom-line, and the page calls itself "The Bottom Line" and "The daily reads".
- The Record page prints its own heading twice.
- Program 5's names (Markets, Flows, Charts, News, Brief, Record, Learn) were scheduled for Sprint 4, but they are the fix for most of this tangle.

## 2. The program: four sprints, one session each

### Sprint 6.1: one look on every page (Opus 5.5, medium)

Apply Program 5's visual system (section 7: the tokens, the three typefaces, paper and designed dark mode, no gradients, no photos, no persona art) to every page: the Board, Whale Watch, the Chart Master, News, the Brief, the Record, Learn, About, every coin page, every story page.

- Remove the gradient wash and the photo hero.
- Retire "Crypto Cronkite" from every title, meta tag, share card and alt text.
- Replace the persona copy with plain copy under the September 16 copy law.
- Enforce the tile law everywhere: no tile carries more than its label, its figure, its window, one since-yesterday line and its source with the stamp.

Proof: every page at 375 and 1440, light and dark, before and after, sent to Jack before any merge.

### Sprint 6.2: the home page, edited (Opus 5.5, medium)

1. One Bitcoin day change. The tape is the only live figure. The narrative band's small line and since yesterday read the Board's stamp and do not print a second day change for Bitcoin.
2. Nothing twice. A reading shown above the fold does not reappear below it. The Board below the fold shows only the readings that are not already above.
3. The tape's chart becomes a chart: the week drawn to scale with a soft fill under the line, the low and high marked on the line itself, the last point dotted, drawn once, never looping.
4. Tiles cut to the law. Explanations move to the Learn page behind "what this means".

### Sprint 6.3: navigation and names (Sonnet 5.5, medium)

Program 5's Sprint 4 navigation, pulled forward:

- The names: Markets (the Board), Flows (Whale Watch), Charts (Chart Master), News, Brief (the Edition, at its existing URL, which never changes), Record, Learn.
- On a phone: a bottom tab bar with Markets, Flows, Charts, News and Brief. Record and Learn go behind search and in the footer.
- One name per thing, everywhere it appears.
- The Record's double heading is fixed.

### Sprint 6.4: the coin page and the Top 100 (Sonnet 5.5, medium)

Program 5's Sprint 3, built on the repaired system:

- Charts: the chart product with 1D, volume, and the 50-day and 200-day lines, at a size and label weight that read on a phone.
- On each coin page: the readings grid, the dated Chart Master card, and the cold-storage card.
- The Top 100: search and the seven-day lines.
- Carried into this sprint: the canary builds its own tree, and Jack's October 7 ruling that the wire stays in re-count order.

### Riding on 6.1: the stale pieces

1. Retire the old calendar. data/week_calendar.json goes; calendar.json is the calendar, and the brief's calendar check reads calendar.json.
2. Date the Chart Master read. The Board's headline read shows its own date and gives way to the Board's own line when it is more than one day old.
3. Check the ETF read against Farside for October 10, and fix the read if it is behind.
