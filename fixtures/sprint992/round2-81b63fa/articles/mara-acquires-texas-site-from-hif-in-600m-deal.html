MARA Acquires Texas Site From HIF in $600M Deal

MARA has acquired a Texas site from HIF in a $600 million deal covering roughly 2 GW of capacity, part of a miner-to-AI pivot. Multiple outlets confirmed the deal, and reporting indicates shares moved around 15%.

By GoCheckMyCrypto

MARA has agreed to acquire a Texas site from HIF in a $600 million deal covering roughly 2 GW of capacity, according to The Defiant, Cointelegraph, and Decrypt.

The acquisition is described as part of a miner-to-AI pivot. Reporting indicates the company's shares moved by roughly 15%; the exact figure varies across outlets.

The key fact

MARA acquired a Texas site from HIF for $600M, covering about 2 GW of capacity.

The take

The deal is confirmed across multiple outlets. The bigger story is miners turning into energy and AI infrastructure companies.
