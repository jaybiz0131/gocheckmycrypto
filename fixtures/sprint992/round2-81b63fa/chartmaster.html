Terms, in plain words

Golden cross / death cross

The 50-day average crossing above the 200-day is a golden cross (trend turning up); crossing below is a death cross. See them drawn live on Price posture.

Stablecoin supply

Stablecoins are dollars staged inside crypto, ready to buy. The supply is the market's fuel gauge.

Whale flows

Coins moving onto exchanges can precede selling; coins withdrawn to cold storage read as accumulation. Follow the money.

Fear & Greed

A 0-100 crowd-mood gauge. Extremes mark crowded emotions, not value. Today's reading.

RSI

Momentum on a 0-100 scale: above 70 runs hot, below 30 runs cold, and strong trends can stay hot for weeks. Current readings.

Exit liquidity

What you become when you chase a pump with no story behind it. The movers board exists so you check before you chase.
