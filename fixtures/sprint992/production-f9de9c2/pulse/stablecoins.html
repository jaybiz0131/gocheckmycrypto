Stablecoins are dollars that already made the jump into crypto. The size of that supply is the market's fuel gauge: money staged to buy, or money heading for the exit.

How to read it

The direction of the line is the story: a growing supply means money is staying in the arena, staged to buy; a shrinking supply means money is leaving crypto entirely. The 30-day change chip gives you the recent lean at a glance.

Stablecoin supply 101

Why it matters

Before anyone can buy crypto at scale, dollars have to enter the system, and they usually arrive as stablecoins. A growing supply is potential demand parked at the door. It does not say when, or whether, that money will actually buy.

Mints and burns

Issuers create (mint) stablecoins when money comes in and destroy (burn) them when it leaves. The biggest mints and burns show up as news in our daily brief, because a billion-dollar mint is a story, not just a statistic.
